Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play all day long. One day, Sue found a big box in her room. She opened it and saw a big, soft pillow. Sue was very happy and excited.
Sue put the pillow on her wall. She thought it would help her reach the pillow. Sue was a bit careless, but she did it. She did not look where she was going. She looked everywhere but could not see the soft pillow. Sue was very sad.
Then, something unexpected happened. A big, soft pillow appeared! It was so soft and fluffy. Sue and her friends played with the pillow all day. They had so much fun together. Sue knew she would always remember this fun day with her new pillow friend.<|endoftext|>
